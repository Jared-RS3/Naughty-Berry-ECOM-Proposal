PROJECT OVERVIEW

The Brief

Naughty Berry's website is a custom React application built around the brand's own identity. It works. The next phase is not a rebuild — it is adding the ability to sell the sweets and candy range online.

Two routes are set out. Both use Shopify as the commerce engine behind the scenes, and both preserve the existing website. They differ only in where the customer does the shopping.

The pop-up, market and events side of the business is unchanged by this work. The shop sells the packaged sweet range — a shelf-stable product that ships far more easily than anything made fresh to order.

→

Every amount shown under Daybreak Investment is a professional setup and development fee. Shopify subscriptions, payment-processing fees, courier charges and email-platform subscriptions are third-party operational costs, payable directly by Naughty Berry. Section 08 sets them out in full.

THE DECISION

Two Routes to
the Same Engine

Shopify sits behind both options. Products, prices, variants, stock and orders are managed in one place either way. The only question is whether customers shop on a connected Shopify store or inside the existing React website.

OPTION ONE · LINKED SHOPIFY STORE

  • A dedicated Shopify store, styled to Naughty Berry's pink, magenta and chocolate
  • "Order Now" buttons on the current site hand off to the store
  • Fastest, most cost-effective route, with no custom code to maintain

OPTION TWO · CUSTOM REACT INTEGRATION

  • The shop is built into the existing React website
  • Customers browse and build a cart without leaving the site
  • They enter Shopify only at secure checkout — one seamless brand

HOW DELIVERY RUNS — THE SAME FOUR STAGES EITHER WAY

  1. 01

    Setup and access

    Shopify, Yoco, The Courier Guy and Mailchimp accounts created or connected. Product information, weights and dimensions loaded.

  2. 02

    Build and configure

    Catalogue, cart and checkout configured — or, under Option Two, the custom React storefront built against the Shopify Storefront API.

  3. 03

    Payments and delivery

    Yoco connected and tested end to end. The Courier Guy plugin installed, shipping zones and the waybill workflow configured.

  4. 04

    Test, train, launch

    The complete order journey tested on mobile and desktop, order-management training delivered, then production launch.

PAYABLE DIRECTLY BY NAUGHTY BERRY

Third-Party
Operational Costs

These are not included in Daybreak's fees. Final amounts depend on the plans selected, sales volume, transaction value, delivery location and provider pricing at the time of activation, so they are described here rather than quoted.

SHOPIFY

A paid subscription is required under both options. The plan depends on whether delivery rates are configured manually or drawn live from The Courier Guy at checkout — an entry-level plan supports fixed or manual rates, and the plugin can still produce waybills from Shopify orders.

SHOPIFY THIRD-PARTY PAYMENT FEE

Shopify may charge an additional transaction fee when an external payment provider such as Yoco is used. The percentage depends on the subscription selected and must be confirmed when the store is created.

YOCO

Yoco charges a processing fee on successful online payments, at a percentage set by Naughty Berry's own account. Daybreak's R2,000 fee covers setup and integration only.

THE COURIER GUY

The plugin is free; delivery is not. Charges depend on the service, distance, parcel weight, dimensions and speed. Naughty Berry may pass the charge to the customer at checkout or absorb part of it as a sales strategy, and the national one-rate schedule applies unless account-specific rates are approved.

MAILCHIMP

Free at a small subscriber volume. A paid plan becomes necessary as the database grows, or if more automation, segmentation or sending capacity is required.

DOMAIN AND HOSTING

Existing hosting and domain stay in place. Option One may use a branded subdomain such as shop.naughtyberry.co.za, which normally runs on the existing domain without buying another. Anything additional is approved before implementation.

→

According to The Courier Guy, live carrier-calculated shipping rates may require a qualifying annually billed Shopify Grow plan. Confirm this before implementation — it affects both the plan chosen and how delivery is priced at checkout.

WHAT THIS PROPOSAL DOES NOT COVER

Scope Conditions

This page is here so that nothing is assumed. The proposal covers up to 10 initial products; more can be loaded at an agreed per-product fee, or within the monthly change allowance.

THE PROPOSAL ASSUMES

  • Product content is supplied complete and usable
  • No subscription or recurring-order system is required
  • No wholesale or multi-vendor functionality is required
  • No loyalty programme, accounting or point-of-sale integration is required
  • The chosen Shopify, Yoco and Courier Guy plans support the functionality described
  • The existing website does not need a major redesign

NOT INCLUDED, IN THE FEE OR THE RETAINER

  • Product photography and copywriting
  • Daily order processing, packing and dispatch
  • Customer-service management
  • Bulk product loading beyond the initial 10
  • Courier charges, payment fees and platform subscriptions
  • Major redesign, new functionality or undocumented custom development
  • Work requiring restricted third-party access

Courier functionality remains subject to The Courier Guy's plugin capabilities, account approval and supported Shopify features. Anything outside the documented scope is assessed and quoted separately.

→

The packaged sweet range is the right product to start with — shelf-stable, and it ships well. Chocolate-coated lines are still heat-sensitive, so confirm packaging and delivery timeframes with The Courier Guy before payment is made, and decide whether summer despatch needs insulated packaging, a hot-weather cut-off or a regional limit. Fresh strawberry products are outside this scope.
