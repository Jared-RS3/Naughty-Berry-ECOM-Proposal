THE AGREEMENT

One Store.
Ready to Sell.

Daybreak will configure and launch a separate branded Shopify store for Naughty Berry, connected to the existing React website through Shop and Order Now links.

Shopify will manage the catalogue, stock, cart, checkout, customer orders and administration. Yoco will process payments, The Courier Guy will support fulfilment, and Mailchimp will support customer marketing.

→

This proposal is for the Shopify option only. The once-off implementation fee is R9,600, plus a monthly retainer of R1,500.

THE CUSTOMER WILL BE ABLE TO

  2. 02Add products and options to a cart
  3. 03Pay securely through Yoco
  4. 04Select available delivery options
  5. 05Receive order and tracking information
  6. 06Join the Naughty Berry mailing list

HOW IT WORKS

From Website
to Checkout

  1. 01

    Discover

    The customer visits the existing Naughty Berry React website and selects Shop or Order Now.

  2. 02

    Shop

    The customer enters the branded Shopify store, browses products and adds items to Shopify's cart.

  3. 03

    Pay

    Shopify checkout collects customer and delivery information and sends payment through Yoco.

  4. 04

    Fulfil

    The order appears in Shopify. Naughty Berry uses The Courier Guy workflow to create the shipment and waybill.

  5. 05

    Follow up

    Order confirmations and available tracking information are sent to the customer, with Mailchimp supporting agreed marketing flows.

AFTER LAUNCH

A Retainer That
Grows With Sales

The initial monthly retainer of R1,500 is deliberately reduced to lower Naughty Berry's cost while the store is launched and its performance is established.

→

Retainer review: March 2027. Six months after the September 2026 proposal baseline, Daybreak and Naughty Berry will review the store's sales performance and agree the ongoing support level.

The review will use an agreed metric discussed and recorded by the parties before or at the review. This may include gross online sales, order volume, transaction frequency, support workload and the technical demands created by the store's performance.

If the store has produced good sales or materially increased the support required, Daybreak will require a larger retainer. The revised retainer will be a minimum of R2,000 per month and may be higher depending on sales performance and support requirements, as discussed and agreed in writing.

The revised amount is not automatic or retrospective. It takes effect only from the date agreed in writing. Until then, the R1,500 retainer remains payable. Any later review or scope increase will also be agreed in writing.
